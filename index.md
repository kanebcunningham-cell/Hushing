# HUSHING

## A Creative Methodology for Painting Place from the Inside

**Against the Picturesque: Upper Wharfedale**

**Kane Cunningham — 2026**

HUSHING is a place-generated creative methodology for abstract landscape painting, developed by British artist Kane Cunningham.

Named after the historic Yorkshire Dales mining process of hushing, the methodology converts accumulated knowledge of place into decisive material action. Geological, historical, social, bodily and art-historical knowledge is researched and internalised before being released through painting.

> The painting is not an appearance of the landscape but an expression of the place.

## The HUSHING sequence

**Research → Earned Visual Language → Accumulation → Impoundment → First Strike → Release → Entrainment → Expenditure → Deposition → Aftermath**

HUSHING proposes that place can remain present in an abstract painting not principally through resemblance, but through the process by which the painting is made. Place determines the conditions through which the work happens, even when the viewer cannot identify the location from its surface.
